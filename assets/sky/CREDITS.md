# Sky credits

`sky_bg.jpg` and `sky_env.hdr` are made from
[Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky)
by Greg Zaal, Poly Haven, public domain (CC0 1.0).

Changes: rotated so the sun sits where the game's sun is, below-horizon half replaced with
haze and ground colors, the sun disk clipped out of the lighting HDR (the game draws the sun
as a separate light with shadows), and resized (4096 × 2048 background, 1024 × 512 HDR).
`sky.json` holds the sun direction, the lighting strength and the haze color.
